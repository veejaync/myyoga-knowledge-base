Flows & Reflections

Explore Surya Namaskara variations and Vinyasa flows as linked sequences of movement and breath. Record what you notice in your practice, questions to revisit, and personal observations as your understanding grows.

A gentle noteTake your time. Let each movement meet a comfortable breath, and adapt or pause whenever you need.

A space for your reflections

After a flow, you might note:

  • Which movements felt steady and comfortable?
  • How did your breath respond as the sequence changed?
  • What would you like to explore or adapt next time?

These are prompts for personal observation—not a test or a requirement.
