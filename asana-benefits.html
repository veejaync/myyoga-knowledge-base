Section 4 · Asana study

Benefits of Different
Asana Categories

Explore what different groups of yoga postures may help you practise and notice. Use this guide as a starting point—each pose and each body is different.

01 Balancing 02 Inversions 03 Core 04 Prone 05 Standing 06 Backbends 07 Forward bends 08 Hip openers 09 Seated 10 Twists 11 Lateral bends

01

Steadiness & focus

  • Practise balance, coordination, and stability.
  • Engage the legs and core.
  • Build concentration and body awareness.
  • Develop confidence through gradual practice.
02

Supported strength

  • Some variations engage the core, arms, and shoulders.
  • Offer a chance to practise balance and coordination.
  • Encourage focus and awareness of body position.
  • Build confidence only with suitable preparation and guidance.
03

Control & support

  • Engage abdominal and trunk muscles.
  • Practise controlled movement and stability.
  • Support awareness of posture and spinal alignment.
  • Challenge balance and coordination in some poses.
04

Back-body awareness

  • Some poses engage the back and other posterior-body muscles.
  • May involve the glutes and hamstrings.
  • Offer gentle opportunities to explore spinal movement.
05

Grounding & alignment

  • Engage the legs and lower body.
  • Practise balance, stability, and coordination.
  • Build awareness of posture and alignment.
  • Some flowing sequences can build stamina.
06

Spaciousness & mobility

  • Explore comfortable spinal extension and mobility.
  • Some poses engage the back muscles.
  • Move the chest, shoulders, and front body through a range of motion.
  • Develop posture and movement awareness.
07

Ease & gradual range

  • May lengthen the hamstrings and other areas along the back of the body.
  • Explore hip movement and comfortable spinal motion.
  • Can offer a quieter focus and inward awareness.
  • Let flexibility develop gradually—never force a stretch.
08

Comfortable range

  • Explore hip mobility and flexibility at a comfortable pace.
  • Move muscles around the hips through varied positions.
  • Adapt seated postures to support your own range of motion.
09

Stability & stillness

  • Explore hip and lower-body mobility.
  • Practise comfortable, upright spinal alignment.
  • Offer a steady base for pranayama or meditation when suitable.
  • Encourage relaxation and body awareness.
10

Rotation & awareness

  • Explore comfortable rotational movement through the spine.
  • Practise posture and body awareness.
  • Coordinate movement with an easy breath.
  • Some variations also move the shoulders or hips.
11

Side-body movement

  • Explore comfortable side-to-side spinal movement.
  • Lengthen the sides of the torso and rib area.
  • Engage the trunk to support the movement.
  • Build awareness of posture and balance.

Practise with care

These are general possibilities, not guaranteed results or medical advice. Benefits depend on the specific pose, how it is practised, and the individual. Use a comfortable range, stop if you feel pain or unwell, and seek qualified guidance before trying inversions or poses that do not feel appropriate for you.
